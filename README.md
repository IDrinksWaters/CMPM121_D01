# D1 starter

You're the king of the milk dragon species, your kingdom has always been peaceful... but not anymore. Scarce resource causes other
kingdoms attempt to take your territories, you must defeat them by recruiting your own kind as soldiers to defend your kingdom...

# How to Run

Install Deno, then open a terminal in the project root and run:

deno task dev

Open the local URL shown in the terminal. Keep the server running while playing.

## How to Play

Click Gain soildiers! to recruit Milk Dragons and increase your total power. Once you have enough power, attack Max, then Salt, and finally Water Bottle.

Attacking spends soldiers to cover the required power, starting with weaker troops. Soldiers are spent as whole units, so an attack may consume slightly more power than required. Each defeated leader unlocks a new soldier type.

Unlocked armies automatically produce soldiers over time. Recruiting enough new Milk Dragons also grants extra soldiers from each unlocked army. These bonus soldiers do not consume Milk Dragons, and unlocked armies keep producing even if their existing soldiers are spent.

## Current Demo

The demo includes recruitment, total power tracking, three leaders to defeat, automatic production, and bonus recruitment. The interface uses English text and buttons without a combat sequence.

Upgrades and saving are not implemented yet. Refreshing the page resets the game.
